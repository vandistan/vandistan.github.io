Vandiino

The Vandiino language [Vandiino: Azak Vandi] is a language isolate native to the island nation of Vandistan. The dialect taught in these articles is the traditional prestige language of the Bellsound region – a neutral register between Bjarkbalai Vandiino and Saratramese Bakiwa.

Older publications referring simply to 'Vandiino' are more accurately classified as describing the Longyearbyen Trade Pidgin (LTP) – a creolised North Germanic dialect which developed under the Russian and Norse occupation of Vandistan. As such, this language is not the native tongue of the Vandiini, but rather of the ethnically diverse Longyearbyen capital region.
